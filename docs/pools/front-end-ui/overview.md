The **Pools Frontend UI** provides a complete interface for managing lending and borrowing activities on the Defactor platform. It is designed as a modular dashboard, giving users visibility into liquidity, collateral, rewards, and risks across multiple pools.  

This section of the documentation covers all user-facing components, from the high-level dashboard to detailed pool interactions. Each page explains both **what is displayed** and **why it matters**, ensuring users understand how to make informed supply and borrow decisions.  

---

## Structure  

The Pools UI is organized into the following sections:  

- **[Dashboard](./pools-frontend-dashboard.md)** – The central overview for all pools. Displays TVL, utilization charts, activity summaries, and active/closed pool tables.  
- **[Supplied](./pools-frontend-supplied.md)** – Focused view of all supply positions. Includes supply vs rewards charts, position health status, stats panels, and management tools.  
- **[Borrowed](./pools-frontend-borrowed.md)** – Focused view of all borrowing positions. Provides repayment tracking, health indicators, collateral management, and borrowing stats.  
- **[Pool Details](./pools-frontend-pool-details.md)** – In-depth page for a specific pool, showing its parameters, supply/borrow panel, utilization stats, and personal supply/borrow positions with management modals.  

## Key Features  

Across the Pools Frontend UI, users can:  

- **Track Liquidity** – View total supplied, borrowed, and available funds across pools or within a single pool.  
- **Monitor Rewards** – Compare rewards vs supplied amounts to evaluate performance.  
- **Assess Health** – Use charts and indicators to check collateralization ratios and avoid liquidation.  
- **Manage Positions** – Supply, withdraw, borrow, repay, or update collateral directly from interactive tables and modals.  
- **Switch Views** – Navigate easily between global dashboards and detailed pool pages.  

## Workflow Benefits  

- **Unified Interface** – Centralized access to all lending and borrowing features.  
- **Real-Time Data** – Live updates on supply, borrow, collateral, and reward values.  
- **Risk Transparency** – Clear warnings, tooltips, and health indicators to prevent liquidation.  
- **Cross-Pool Management** – Supply or borrow across multiple pools without leaving the interface.  
- **User-Friendly Controls** – Sortable tables, charts, and modals streamline management actions.  

> The Pools Frontend UI provides both **high-level visibility** and **granular control**, making it the key interface for users to engage with Defactor’s lending and borrowing ecosystem.
