# My Templates

The My Templates section provides a comprehensive template management system for streamlined asset tokenization. This interface allows users to create custom templates for repeated asset types and access pre-built community templates for common asset categories.

---

## Overview

Templates serve as standardized frameworks that simplify the tokenization process by pre-configuring common asset parameters, compliance requirements, and structural elements. This reduces setup time and ensures consistency across similar asset types.

### Key Features

- **Custom Template Creation** - Build reusable templates for your specific asset types
- **Community Templates** - Access professionally designed templates for common assets
- **Asset Category Coverage** - Templates for diverse asset classes including financial instruments, real estate, commodities, and luxury goods
- **Streamlined Workflow** - Reduce tokenization time through pre-configured settings

---

## My Templates Section

**Create Template Card**
- Click the "+" button to build a new custom template
- **Purpose**: Build a custom template for easy asset creation
- **Benefits**: Streamline future tokenization processes for similar assets

The interface displays your saved custom templates in a grid format, showing:

## Template Selection Process

### Choosing a Template

1. **Assess Your Asset Type** - Identify which category best matches your asset
2. **Review Template Features** - Examine included parameters and compliance elements
3. **Consider Customization Needs** - Determine if additional modifications are required
4. **Select and Configure** - Choose template and adjust specific parameters

### Template Customization Workflow

1. Browse available pre-built templates in the My Templates section
2. Select "Copy Template" for the template you want to modify
3. Click the actions menu (⋯) next to your copied template
4. Choose "Edit" to customize the template according to your specific needs

The Templates section provides an organized, step-by-step approach that guides you through the entire asset configuration process, whether you're starting fresh, using existing templates, or customizing pre-built options to fit your requirements.

### Understanding Asset Data Types

Before proceeding with asset creation, it's important to understand the two main categories of asset data:

**Static Data:**
- Information that remains **constant** throughout the asset's lifecycle
- Examples: Asset name, commodity ID, legal documentation, initial valuation
- Cannot be changed after the asset is created
- Forms the foundation of your asset's identity and legal structure

**Dynamic Data:**
- Information that **changes over time** and requires regular updates
- Examples: Current market price, inventory levels, performance metrics, location data
- Updated periodically through data feeds, manual input, or automated systems
- Reflects the real-time status and value of your underlying asset

Understanding this distinction helps you choose the right template and configure your asset properly. Static data templates are ideal for assets with fixed characteristics, while dynamic data templates suit assets requiring ongoing data updates and real-time tracking.
